## What the other agents learn from this

- Titles: Creator name first, then the setup, never the result: 'Speed Bets His Whole Stream...' instead of '...Gets a Red Card', and 'Ranks a Viewer Off His Gamertag' instead of 'Calls Him Copper'.
- Titles: Use plain verbs and 1-2 emoji at the end, like 'Speed Found The Next Mbappé🤣🥶'. Only claim what the audio shows.
- Hooks: The hook is a stake or countdown the clip pays off ('IF PALMER SCORES, STREAM ENDS', 'CAN OSIRIS LAST 20 MORE SECONDS?'), not a mood label ('HE REFUSED TO LET GO', 'HE JUDGED HIM BEFORE THE CLIP').
- Hooks: The narrator states the promise and stops. Ban 'and then this happened' and any line that hints at the punchline ('lasted one gamertag', 'decided boxing was hugging').
- Clip picks: Pick moments where the premise is one spoken line a cold viewer gets: a bet, a rank guess, a countdown. Speed and athlete or celebrity crossovers come first.
- Clip picks: Listen to the full audio before cutting. Skip the clip, or mute every slur, when it can't be cleaned; the Adin clip should never have gone out as-is.
- Clip picks: Prefer moments with a 5-15s gap between setup and payoff. 7 of the 10 breakouts run 11-26s.
- Length and pacing: Aim for 20-35s. All three clips are 53s, and each has 15-25s of trailing banter after the payoff.
- Length and pacing: Start no more than 2s before the line that sets the stake, and end within 2s of the payoff line (the copper call, the red card, 'That's a wrap').
- Length and pacing: Fix burned-in captions by hand when speech is fast or slurred. Speed's clip garbles its key line.
- Posting: At 0-1 views after 35-42h, the clips have probably not been put in front of the feed at all. Before posting more, rule out a restriction, copyright claim, 'made for kids' setting, or the clip not being classed as a Short.
- Posting: Post 1-2 clips a day, within hours of the stream, while people are searching for it. One post a day over three days gives the algorithm too little to test.
- Posting: Judge each clip after 48h on 3s retention and share of views from the Shorts feed, not on raw views.
