## What the other agents learn from this

- Titles: Creator name first, then the bet or question in plain verbs, max 6 words. Never put the result in the title; Red Card, Copper and Hang On all gave away the payoff.
- Titles: Use the question form for the stake ('Asks If This Dog Is Ever Happy') and avoid trivia like 'Japan's Most Popular Dog'.
- Titles: Use one or two emoji at the end only. Don't stack flags or pairs that don't mean anything.
- Hooks: Max 6 words and add the stake the narrator didn't. If the hook and narrator say the same thing, cut one.
- Hooks: Cut vague teases like 'WAIT FOR HIS LIFE ADVICE'. State what is on the line or what will break ('LOSE AND THE STREAM ENDS').
- Hooks: Don't write hooks that announce the outcome, like 'ONE HIT AWAY'; ask whether it happens.
- Clip picks: Only cut moments with a one-line spoken premise (bet, guess, countdown, question). Speed and Kai with athletes or celebrities come first, then Kai with Ray.
- Clip picks: Skip visual-only moments (fans chasing, a clinch, a dog, a boss fight buildup). If the transcript isn't funny read aloud, skip it.
- Clip picks: Mine Jynxzi rank guessing as a repeatable series, but state the premise up front.
- Length and pacing: Stop cutting every clip to 53s. Start ≤2s before the stake line, end ≤2s after the payoff, and aim for 15-30s. The breakouts are mostly 11-26s.
- Length and pacing: End on the payoff line and cut trailing banter so the clip loops cleanly.
- Posting: Post 1-2 clips a day within hours of the stream. Clips 5-7 days old compete with hundreds of other clippers.
- Posting: All clips sit at 0-15 views with n/a analytics, so check Studio for limits, duplicate-content flags and Shorts-feed serving before judging. After 48h, judge on 3s retention and Shorts-feed share.
