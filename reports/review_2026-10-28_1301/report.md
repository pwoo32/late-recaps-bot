## What the other agents learn from this

- Titles: Creator name first, then the setup, never the result. Speed, Jynxzi and Adin all gave away the payoff in the title ('Red Card', 'Copper', 'Hang On').
- Titles: Sell the joke, not the trivia. 'Asks If This Dog Is Ever Sad' beats 'Japan's Most Popular Dog'. Plain verbs, 1-2 emoji at the end, and only claims the audio backs up.
- Hooks: Write the hook as the question or stake the clip answers within 15s ('IF PALMER SCORES, STREAM ENDS', 'GUESS HIS RANK FROM HIS NAME'), never a mood label.
- Hooks: The narrator states the promise and stops. Ban 'and then this happened' and any line that hints at the punchline. If the narrator line repeats the hook text, cut one of them.
- Clip picks: Pick moments whose premise is one spoken line a cold viewer gets: a bet, a guess, a countdown, a question. Athlete or celebrity crossovers with Speed and Kai first. The Speed vs. Palmer clip is the only one that got any views.
- Clip picks: Listen to the full clip before cutting. Two of these six clips (Kai/Ray and Adin) shipped with unmuted n-words. If a slur can't be muted without killing the payoff, skip the clip.
- Clip picks: Skip moments that only work visually or depend on crosstalk (fans chasing Kai, breed talk). If the transcript doesn't make sense read out loud, a scroller won't get it either.
- Length and pacing: All six clips are exactly 53s, which looks like a template, not an edit. Cut each clip to its own length: start ≤2s before the stake line, end ≤2s after the payoff. Most of these come out at 18-30s.
- Length and pacing: Every clip has 15-25s of trailing banter after the payoff. Ending on the payoff line ('That's a wrap', 'already taken', 'GG's') is what drives loops.
- Length and pacing: Hand-fix burned-in captions on the key line whenever the speech is fast or slurred.
- Posting: Six clips at 0-15 views with n/a analytics across the board after 107-162h is a distribution problem, not just a clip problem. Fix that before judging any pick.
- Posting: Post 1-2 clips a day, within hours of the stream, so you catch people searching for it. Clips from streams days old compete with hundreds of other clippers.
- Posting: After 48h, judge each clip on 3s retention and share of views from the Shorts feed, and only once Studio actually shows those numbers.
