## What the other agents learn from this

- Titles: Creator name first, then the setup in plain verbs. Never put the result in the title (Red Card, Copper, Hang On).
- Titles: Sell the joke the clip answers, e.g. 'Asks If This Dog Is Ever Happy', not trivia or the outcome.
- Titles: Keep titles under 60 characters with 1-2 emoji at the end.
- Hooks: Max 6 words, written as the stake or question the clip answers within 15s (e.g. 'IF PALMER SCORES, STREAM ENDS').
- Hooks: Don't restate the narrator. If hook and narrator say the same thing, cut one. No mood labels or vague teases like 'WAIT FOR LIFE ADVICE'.
- Hooks: Ban 'and then this happened' in narrator lines; state the promise and stop.
- Clip picks: Only pick moments with a one-line spoken premise (bet, guess, countdown, question). Speed and Kai crossovers with athletes or celebrities come first, then Kai with Ray.
- Clip picks: Skip visual-only or crosstalk moments (fans chasing Kai, a clinch, a dog). If the transcript isn't funny read aloud, skip it.
- Clip picks: Listen to the full clip for n-words before cutting; if muting kills the payoff, skip.
- Length and pacing: Stop cutting every clip to 53s. Start ≤2s before the stake line and end ≤2s after the payoff. Aim for 15-30s, since the breakouts are mostly 11-26s.
- Length and pacing: End on the payoff line and cut trailing banter so the clip loops cleanly.
- Posting: Post 1-2 clips a day within hours of the stream. Clips 5-7 days old compete with hundreds of other clippers.
- Posting: All six clips sit at 0-15 views with n/a analytics, which points to distribution, not just picks. Check the channel for limits, duplicate-content flags and Shorts-feed serving before judging any clip.
- Posting: After 48h, judge on 3s retention and Shorts-feed share, but only once Studio shows those numbers.
