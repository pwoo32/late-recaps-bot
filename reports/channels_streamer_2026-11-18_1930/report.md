## Your plan to replicate it

1. **Niche:** Streamer clips (Twitch/Kick/YouTube live). Pick 3-5 of the sources above that allow clipping or pay for it.
2. **Volume:** post 1-2 Shorts a day, with your best one around 18:00 UTC.
3. **Length:** aim for the 0-15s range
4. **Titles:** match the openers above; keep them around 44 characters.
5. **Make each clip yours:** your own captions, a hook line in the first second, cuts and commentary or context. Reuploads of other channels' clips get copyright strikes and are excluded from monetization as reused content.
6. **Money:** apply to clipping campaigns for your sources first; they pay per view from day one, while Shorts ads need YPP and (from Feb 1, 2027) 10M qualified Shorts views per 90 days.
7. **Rerun weekly** to track which channels are rising and which formats are working now.
