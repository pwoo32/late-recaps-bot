## How to use this

- Clip what the top niche's recurring topics are about, from long-form sources that allow clipping (many podcasts and streamers run paid clipping programs).
- Add commentary, captions or context to every clip. Plain re-uploads get flagged as reused content and are excluded from YouTube monetization.
- From Feb 1, 2027, Shorts ad payouts need 10M qualified Shorts views per 90 days, so early on, clipping campaigns usually pay far more than Shorts ads.
- Money figures use third-party 2026 RPM/CPM estimates in niches.py; replace them with your own numbers once you have them.
