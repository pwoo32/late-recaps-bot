Studied 20 channels from `channels_streamer_2026-11-14_1708`, 117 of their Shorts and 7369 comment threads. Found 26 replies by the channels themselves and 5 of their own top-level comments. Quota used: 364 units. Guide written by: gemini.

All of this is correlation from public comments, not a controlled test. Read it as what winning channels do, not proof that doing it causes the views.

## Reply guide (fed to the channel activity agent)

Top streamer clip channels rarely reply to viewers, with 13 out of 20 channels staying completely silent and the overall channel reply rate sitting around 1%. However, pinning a strong top comment strongly correlates with higher viewer engagement, driving a 1.62x comment lift (lift_top: 1.62 vs 1.0 baseline across 117 videos). When top channels do pin first comments, they heavily rely on open-ended questions (60%) and emojis (100%) to kickstart discussion.

**Replies**

- Keep channel replies extremely rare and concise; across 26 analyzed replies, the median word count is just 1.0 (reply_words: 1.0).
- Maintain a fast response time when replying to early comments (reply_minutes median: 128.5).
- Use single emojis or very short reactions frequently, as emoji usage appears in 73% of channel replies (reply_traits.emoji: 0.73).
- Ask short, provocative follow-up questions to prompt viewer responses (reply_traits.question: 0.23).
- Keep the tone casual and lowercase when typing brief texts (reply_traits.lowercase: 0.19).

**Pinned first comment**

- Pin an open-ended question about the clip to trigger viewer debates (top_traits.question: 0.60, lift_top: 1.62).
- Include an expressive emoji in every pinned comment (top_traits.emoji: 1.0).
- Keep pinned comments brief, aiming for around 6 words (top_words: 6).

**Avoid**

- Avoid asking viewers to subscribe in comment replies (sub_ask: 0.0 in top comments).
- Do not post external links (link: 0.0 across top traits).
- Never pretend to be the featured streamer or engage in hostile arguments (hostile: 0.0).
