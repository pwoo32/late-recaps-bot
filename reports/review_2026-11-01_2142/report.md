## What the other agents learn from this

- Titles: Creator name first, then a bet or question in plain verbs, and never the result. Add a named guest or celebrity when one exists.
- Titles: Use 1-2 emoji at the end and keep it under 60 characters. Don't claim more than the clip shows ('Taller Than Everyone').
- Hooks: Max 6 words, and state what is on the line ('LOSE AND THE STREAM ENDS'). Don't repeat the narrator or the title.
- Hooks: Never ship without a hook and context line (OVl3TBtcYHo and JGopxMMkJo8 did).
- Clip picks: Pick moments with a spoken one-line premise: a bet, guess, argument or question. Skip walk-ins, dogs, clinches, boss-fight buildups and tip lectures.
- Clip picks: One clip per moment, since four dubstep cuts split the audience. Keep the version with a named guest.
- Clip picks: Prioritise Speed and Kai with celebrities, Kai with Rakai or Ray, and Jynxzi rank guessing as a series.
- Length and pacing: Aim for 15-30s, since most breakouts run 11-26s. Start ≤2s before the stake line and end ≤2s after the payoff.
- Length and pacing: End on the payoff line and cut trailing banter so the clip loops.
- Posting: Post 1-2 clips a day within hours of the stream, since clips older than a few days get 0-15 views.
- Posting: Confirm that scheduled clips actually go public. After 48h, judge on 3s retention and Shorts-feed share, and check Studio for limits or duplicate-content flags.
