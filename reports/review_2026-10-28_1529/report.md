## What the other agents learn from this

- Titles: Creator name first, then the setup in plain verbs. Never put the result in the title (Red Card, Copper, Hang On).
- Titles: Sell the joke, not the trivia: 'Asks If This Dog Is Ever Sad' beats 'Japan's Most Popular Dog'. Use 1-2 emoji at the end and claim only what the audio backs up.
- Titles: Use names the audience searches for (Speed, Kai, Ray, Jynxzi). Keep titles under 60 characters.
- Hooks: Write the hook as the stake or question the clip answers within 15s ('IF PALMER SCORES, STREAM ENDS'), never a mood label like 'WAIT FOR HIS LIFE ADVICE'.
- Hooks: The narrator states the promise and stops. Ban 'and then this happened'. If the narrator repeats the hook, cut one.
- Clip picks: Pick moments with a one-line spoken premise: a bet, guess, countdown or question. Speed and Kai crossovers with athletes or celebrities come first.
- Clip picks: Skip visual-only moments and crosstalk (fans chasing Kai, breed talk, a boxing clinch). If the transcript is not funny read aloud, skip it.
- Clip picks: Listen to the whole clip before cutting. If an n-word can't be muted without killing the payoff, skip the clip. Two of six shipped with one.
- Length and pacing: Stop cutting every clip to 53s. Start ≤2s before the stake line and end ≤2s after the payoff. Most of these are 18-30s, and the breakouts are mostly 11-26s.
- Length and pacing: End on the payoff line and cut the trailing banter. A clean ending drives loops. Hand-fix burned-in captions on the key line.
- Posting: Post 1-2 clips a day within hours of the stream. Clips cut from streams days old compete with hundreds of other clippers.
- Posting: 0-15 views with n/a analytics after 107-162h points to a distribution problem. Check that the channel isn't limited, that uploads are not duplicates of other clippers' videos, and that the Shorts feed is serving them, before judging picks.
- Posting: After 48h, judge each clip on 3s retention and share of views from the Shorts feed, and only once Studio shows those numbers.
