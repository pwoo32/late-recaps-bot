## What the other agents learn from this

- Titles: Creator name first, setup in plain verbs, no result. Cut 'Red Card', 'Copper', 'Hang On' style spoilers.
- Titles: Sell the joke, not the trivia: 'Asks If This Dog Is Ever Happy' beats 'Japan's Most Popular Dog'.
- Titles: Keep titles under 60 characters with 1-2 emoji at the end, and use searchable names (Speed, Kai, Ray, Jynxzi).
- Hooks: Write the hook as the stake or question the clip answers within 15s, like 'IF PALMER SCORES, STREAM ENDS'. Never use a mood label.
- Hooks: Max 6 words, and don't repeat the narrator. If the hook and narrator say the same thing, cut one.
- Hooks: Ban 'and then this happened' in narrator lines. State the promise and stop.
- Clip picks: Only pick moments with a one-line spoken premise (bet, guess, countdown, question). Speed and Kai crossovers with athletes or celebrities come first.
- Clip picks: Skip visual-only moments and crosstalk (fans chasing Kai, a boxing clinch, a dog). If the transcript isn't funny read aloud, skip it.
- Clip picks: Prefer Ray clips: the top breakout in the niche is Ray, and Kai plus Ray is the strongest pairing.
- Clip picks: Listen to the full clip for n-words before cutting. If muting kills the payoff, skip the clip.
- Length and pacing: Stop cutting every clip to 53s. Start ≤2s before the stake line and end ≤2s after the payoff. Aim for 15-30s; the breakouts are mostly 11-26s.
- Length and pacing: End on the payoff line and cut trailing banter, since a clean ending drives loops.
- Posting: Post 1-2 clips a day within hours of the stream. Clips 5-7 days old compete with hundreds of other clippers.
- Posting: 0-15 views with n/a analytics after 107-165h points to a distribution problem. Check the channel for limits, duplicate-content flags, and whether the Shorts feed serves these before judging picks.
- Posting: After 48h, judge each clip on 3s retention and Shorts-feed share, but only once Studio shows those numbers.
