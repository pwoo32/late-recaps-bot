## What the other agents learn from this

- Titles: Put the creator first, then the bet or question in plain verbs, max 6 words. Never put the result in the title: 'Red Card', 'Copper' and 'Hang On' all gave away the payoff.
- Titles: Phrase the stake as a question ('Can Kai Get Ray Through?'). Skip trivia ('Japan's Most Popular Dog') and vague teases ('Can't Believe What Happens').
- Titles: Use one or two emoji at the end only. Drop stacks like 😭❤️‍🩹 and flags.
- Hooks: Max 6 words, and add the stake the narrator didn't. If the hook and narrator say the same thing, cut one.
- Hooks: Ask whether it happens; never announce the outcome ('ONE HIT AWAY', 'HIS NAME IS REALLY UP THERE'). State what's on the line ('LOSE AND THE STREAM ENDS').
- Hooks: Cut vague teases like 'WAIT FOR HIS LIFE ADVICE'. Every clip needs a hook and context line: OVl3TBtcYHo and JGopxMMkJo8 shipped with neither.
- Clip picks: Only cut moments with a one-line spoken premise (bet, guess, countdown, question, argument). Speed or Kai with athletes and celebrities come first, then Kai with Ray.
- Clip picks: Skip visual-only moments (crowds, dogs, clinches, boss-fight buildups, walk-ins). If it isn't funny read aloud, skip it.
- Clip picks: One cut per moment. Three dubstep clips of the same scene is the clearest waste in this batch; pick the one with the named guest.
- Clip picks: Mine Jynxzi rank guessing as a series, but state the premise in the first line.
- Length and pacing: Stop cutting every clip to 53s. Start ≤2s before the stake line, end ≤2s after the payoff, and aim for 15-30s. Most breakouts run 11-26s.
- Length and pacing: End on the payoff line and cut trailing banter so the clip loops.
- Posting: Post 1-2 clips a day within hours of the stream. Clips 5-7 days old compete with hundreds of other clippers and sit at 0 views.
- Posting: Every clip sits at 0-18 views with n/a analytics, so check Studio for limits, duplicate-content flags and Shorts-feed serving. After 48h, judge on 3s retention and Shorts-feed share.
