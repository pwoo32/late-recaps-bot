## What the other agents learn from this

- Titles: Creator name first, then the setup in plain verbs. Keep the result out of the title (Red Card, Copper, Hang On all gave it away).
- Titles: Phrase the title as the bet or question the clip answers, e.g. 'Asks If This Dog Is Ever Happy'. Never use trivia like 'Japan's Most Popular Dog'.
- Hooks: Max 6 words, stated as a stake the clip answers within 15s. Cut vague teases like 'WAIT FOR HIS LIFE ADVICE'.
- Hooks: Hook and narrator must not say the same thing. The narrator sets up the premise and the hook adds the stake, or cut one.
- Clip picks: Only cut moments with a one-line spoken premise (bet, guess, countdown, question). Speed and Kai crossovers with athletes or celebrities come first, then Kai with Ray.
- Clip picks: Skip visual-only moments (fans chasing Kai, a clinch, a dog). If the transcript isn't funny read aloud, skip it.
- Clip picks: Mine Jynxzi rank guessing as a repeatable format, but only when the premise is stated up front.
- Length and pacing: Stop cutting every clip to 53s. Start ≤2s before the stake line and end ≤2s after the payoff. Aim for 15-30s, since the breakouts are mostly 11-26s.
- Length and pacing: End on the payoff line and cut trailing banter so the clip loops cleanly.
- Posting: Post 1-2 clips a day within hours of the stream. Clips 5-7 days old compete with hundreds of other clippers.
- Posting: All six clips sit at 0-15 views with n/a analytics, which points to distribution. Check Studio for limits, duplicate-content flags and Shorts-feed serving before judging any clip.
- Posting: After 48h, judge on 3s retention and Shorts-feed share, but only once Studio shows those numbers.
