## Reply guide (fed to the channel activity agent)

Top streamer clip channels heavily rely on posting top/first comments to drive viewer engagement, which correlates with a 1.62 lift in comments per view (lift_top: 1.62 vs lift_no_top: 0.98). Successful channel interactions are extremely brief, averaging 1.5 words per reply and 6 words for top comments. Engaging viewers through questions is key, as 50% of channel replies and 60% of top comments feature questions to spark thread activity.

**Replies**

- Keep replies ultra-short, targeting an average of 1.5 words and staying under 3 words (reply_words: 1.5, reply_words_p75: 3).
- Ask open questions in 50% of your replies to encourage further conversation (picked_question: 0.14 vs passed_question: 0.02; reply_traits.question: 0.5).
- Use emojis frequently, included in 62% of direct replies (reply_traits.emoji: 0.62).
- Prioritize replying to comments with higher existing engagement and likes (picked_likes: 2 vs passed_likes: 1).
- Skip plain laugh-only responses to incoming comments (passed_laugh: 0.31 vs picked_laugh: 0.0).
- Keep responses casual using lowercase text when fitting (reply_traits.lowercase: 0.25).

**Pinned first comment**

- Always leave a top comment on new uploads to boost overall engagement (lift_top: 1.62 vs lift_no_top: 0.98).
- Ask a direct question about the clip in your top comment to trigger replies (top_traits.question: 0.6).
- Include at least one emoji in every top comment (top_traits.emoji: 1.0).
- Keep top comments concise, averaging around 6 words total (top_words: 6).

**Avoid**

- Do not post long, wordy replies exceeding 3 words (reply_words_p75: 3).
- Avoid leaving empty laugh-only reactions without substance or follow-ups (passed_laugh: 0.31).
- Never ask for subscribers or post channel links in comments (reply_traits.sub_ask: 0.0, reply_traits.link: 0.0).
- Do not post top comments without questions or emotional hooks, as generic statements fail to drive thread activity (top_traits.question: 0.6).
