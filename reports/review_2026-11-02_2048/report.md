- **Affiliate links in the channel bio and pinned comments**: Links in Shorts descriptions aren't clickable. Put one link hub in the channel header and pin a comment on breakout clips (gaming chairs, headsets, the games streamed, Amazon Associates or a game-key store). Only worth it once clips pass ~50k views.
